Today's Science Bellringer

Topic: Newton's Laws & Mechanics

A 1,200 kg car accelerates from rest to 20 m/s in 5 seconds. What net force was exerted on the car?

Solution & Answer

F = m × a. Acceleration = (20 - 0) / 5 = 4 m/s². Force = 1,200 kg × 4 m/s² = 4,800 N.
