A model for students, teachers, and administrators

AI does not remove the work.It moves it.

When AI helps with a piece of work, the work does not disappear. Some of it moves to Checking. This model shows where it goes and what to do about it.

Start with the stagesRun the lab

Every piece of work has three stages

Plan. Do. Check.

AI can decrease Planning and Doing. Some of the work moves to Checking. Completion time can go up, down, or stay the same.
