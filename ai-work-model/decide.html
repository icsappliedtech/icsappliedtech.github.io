Four questions decide what to do

Answer the four questions about a piece of work. The rule tells you what to do.

1. How large is it?
2. How difficult is it to check?
3. What does a mistake cost?
4. Is the work the point?
If the work is how you learn, the work is the point.
What to do

The decision rule

  • The work is the point: do it yourself first.
  • Easy to check, cheap mistake: use AI. Check lightly.
  • Easy to check, costly mistake: use AI. Check every time.
  • Difficult to check, cheap mistake: use AI. Spot check.
  • Difficult to check, costly mistake: do it yourself, or use AI with a source and an expert check.
