Teach it in three lessons

Each lesson uses one lab test and ends with one student rule.

What students get

Students learn to expect checking, to match the amount of checking to the cost of a mistake, and to protect the work that builds their skill.

These are aims. There is no evidence yet that the lessons change what students do. The lab collects the first data.

Run it as a PD session

A suggested 45 minutes. Everyone uses their own work.

  1. 5 min. Show the model on the home page.
  2. 10 min. Sort your own work on Stages.
  3. 10 min. Run two real examples through Decide.
  4. 10 min. Time one task on the Time test.
  5. 10 min. Pick the first lesson you will run.

Where it sits in AI literacy

AI literacy frameworks cover more: how AI works, bias, ethics, and how AI is designed. This model covers one part. It covers working with AI output on real work. Two frameworks to read next are on the Sources page.
