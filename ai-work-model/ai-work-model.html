AI Moves Work Model

AI does not remove the work.It moves it.

AI can decrease Planning and Doing. Some of the work moves to Checking. Completion time can go up, down, or stay the same.

Three types of work

Work has three stages: Plan. Do. Check.

Checking has two questions

Accuracy

Is it right?

Quality

Is it good?

Good needs domain knowledge and expertise.
