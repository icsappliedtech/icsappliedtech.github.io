Plan. Do. Check.

Every piece of work has three stages. AI can decrease Planning and Doing. Some of the work moves to Checking. Completion time can go up, down, or stay the same.

Plan

Decide what you need and how you will get it.

Do

Make the thing. Write it, build it, solve it, or prompt for it.

Check

Decide whether the thing is right and whether it is good.
